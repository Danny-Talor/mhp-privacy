MHP Privacy Policy

Developer: Danny-Talor
Last updated: 13 November 2026

About this app

MHP is an independent number-suggestion app developed by Danny-Talor. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Mifal HaPais. It does not sell tickets, accept bets, handle payments, or award prizes. Historical frequencies do not predict future draws or improve the odds of winning.

Data handled by MHP

MHP generates suggestions on your device using draw statistics bundled with the app. It does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your name, email address, location, contacts, generated numbers, or device identifiers to the developer. The app includes no advertising, analytics, or developer-operated tracking service.

Local information and retention

Generated cards are held in memory while you use a game. They are cleared when you leave that game and are not uploaded or saved as a history. Draw statistics are part of the app package. There is no developer-held user account or generated-card history to delete. You can remove the app and its local app data through Android settings.

Google Play and your device

Google Play and Android may independently handle installation, update, diagnostics, or other platform information under their own privacy settings and policies. This policy describes MHP's own behavior and does not replace Google's privacy policy.

Contact and external websites

If you contact dannytalor96@gmail.com, your email address and the content you send will be used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request; you can ask for its deletion. Opening an email app or a developer website is optional and is subject to that service's own privacy policy.

Security and children

MHP does not operate a server that stores your generated cards or personal information. The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. The current version is available within MHP. For privacy questions, contact Danny-Talor at dannytalor96@gmail.com.
